Let $v \in V$, where $V$ is a vector space. Suppose $w_1$ and $w_2$ are additive inverses of $v$. Then:

$v + w_1 = 0$ and $v + w_2 = 0$

Adding $v$ to both sides of the first equation:

$$v + (v + w_1) = v + 0 \implies 2v + w_1 = v$$
$$w_1 = v - 2v = -v$$

Similarly, from the second equation:

$$v + (v + w_2) = v + 0 \implies 2v + w_2 = v$$
$$w_2 = v - 2v = -v$$

Therefore, $w_1 = w_2 = -v$. Hence, the additive inverse is unique.
