# Overview

Armed with an understanding of the evolution of the scale facor and the densitites of constituents in the universe, we can extrapolate backwards to explore phenomena at early times. 

When the universe was hot and dense, it was impossible for the matter to form in shape of atoms. Due to the high energy photons, any formed atom or even nuclei would be immediately destroyed. As the universe cooled well below the bound energy of nuclei, light atoms were formed, this process is known as Big Bang Nucleosynthesis.

Knowing the conditions of the early universe and the relevant nuclear cross-sections, we can calculate the expected primordial abundances of all the elements.
