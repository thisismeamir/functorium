# Fine and Coarse Topologies

Sometimes it is useful to compare different Topologies on the same set. Given two topologies $\mathcal{T}_{1}$ and $\mathcal{T}_{2}$ on a set $X$, we say:

**$\mathcal{T}_{1}$ is finer than $\mathcal{T}_{2}$ if $\mathcal{T}_{1} \supseteq \mathcal{T}_{2}$**.

**$\mathcal{T}_{1}$ is coarser than $\mathcal{T}_{2}$ if $\mathcal{T}_{1} \subseteq \mathcal{T}_{2}$**.

The terminology is meant to suggest the picture of a subset that is open in a coarser topology being further subdivided into smaller open subsets in a finer topology.
