# Introduction

In this little draft I'll try to talk to myself through the idea of functorium and how can I organize my readings, thoughts and ideas into a concrete body of knowledge.

The first thing is that I always need a place to be free and without risk, or worrying about the chaos. The `draft` folder is precisely given to me to write whatever I want, whenever I want, without consequences. In this folder I can write everything from a simple note on something, or a draft of a paper or article. 

`logs` are notes that I'd open some days to express something about the situation I'm in, the progress I'm having, or the issues I'm facing. That is an internal dialog for me. Something to read later on and find light for the rest of the process.

`models` is a folder that is organized, structured and contains well-written documents. That is my knowledge hub, my personal wiki. Something that I read and write to keep my knowledge alive and well structured all the time.

Each model is a specific subject that I'm facing. But they can contain more models in them instead of a single one.

# Models

A model is a directory under the models directory. It contains information and knowledge regarding a single subject. For example topology.

Inside these folders we have the following
1. `Model of <topic-name>`: This markdown can be an entry point into the topic. They mostly start with a brief followed by a reading list on the topic.
2. `atoms/<atom-name>`: This directory contains atoms of knowledge in that subject.
3. `List of <list-name>`: This one is just a list of gatherings in a topic. It can be videos, links, materials, or more notes. These notes can be a good place to dive very deeper into the subject. As these are mostly investigative routines and lists of things that one would come across during the investigation of that subject.
