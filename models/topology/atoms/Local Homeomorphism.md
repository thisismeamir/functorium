# Definition

There is a generalization of homeomorphism that is often useful. We say that a map $f: X\to Y$ between topological spaces is a ***local homeomorphism*** if every point $x \in X$ has a neighborhood $U\subseteq X$ such that $f(U)$ is an open subset of $Y$ and $f|_{U}:U \to f(U)$ is a homeomorphism.

Properties of a local homeomorphism:

- Every homeomorphism is a local homeomorphism.
- Every local homeomorphism is continuous and open.
- Every bijective local homeomorphism is a homeomorphism.``
