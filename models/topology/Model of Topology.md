Roughly speaking, topology is the branch of mathematics that is concerned with properties of sets that are unchanged by "continuous deformations". Somewhat more accurately, a *topological property is one that is preserved by homeomorphisms*.

- **Continuity and Homeomorphisms:** Explains continuity of functions and the concept of homeomorphism as a structural equivalence.
    - **Definition of Continuity:** Defines continuous functions in terms of open sets or epsilon-delta definitions.
    - **Properties of Continuous Functions:** Discusses properties like preservation of connectedness, compactness, and homeomorphims.
    - **Homeomorphism Definition & Examples:** Formally defines homeomorphism and provides examples demonstrating structural equivalence.
    - **Relationship between Continuity and Homeomorphisms:** Explores how homeomorphisms imply continuity and vice versa.

- **Connectedness and Compactness:** Discusses properties related to connectedness and compactness in topological spaces.
    - **Connectedness Definition & Types:** Defines connected spaces and distinguishes between path-connectedness and weak connectedness.
    - **Compactness Definition & Properties:** Defines compact spaces using open covers and explores their properties (e.g., closed sets are compact).
    - **Relationship Between Connectedness and Compactness:** Explores the interplay between these two concepts (e.g., compactness in Hausdorff spaces implies connectedness).
    - **Tychonoff Theorem:** Introduces Tychonoff's theorem regarding compactness of infinite products.

- **Product and Quotient Topologies:** Covers constructions involving product and quotient spaces.
    - **Product Topology Definition:** Defines the topology on the product of two or more topological spaces.
    - **Quotient Topology Definition:** Describes how to construct a quotient space from a topological space and an equivalence relation.
    - **Universal Properties of Product and Quotient Spaces:** Discusses the universal mapping properties associated with these constructions.
    - **Examples of Product and Quotient Spaces:** Provides concrete examples illustrating product and quotient spaces.

- **Advanced Topics in Topology:** Delves into more complex areas like homotopy theory, homology, and manifolds.
    - **Homotopy Theory Basics:** Introduces the concepts of homotopy, homotopy groups, and fundamental group.
    - **Homology Theory Introduction:** Explains the basics of singular homology and its applications to classifying topological spaces.
    - **Manifolds: Definition & Classification:** Defines manifolds (topological and differentiable) and discusses their classification.
    - **Fiber Bundles Overview:** Introduces fiber bundles as a way to relate different topological spaces.
