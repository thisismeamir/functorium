# Completely Hausdorff Space

A completely Hausdorff space, or functionally Hausdorff space, is a topological space in which any two distinct points can be separated by a continuous function. 

Suppose that $X$ is a topological space. Let $x,y \in X$.
We say that $x$ and $y$ can be separated by a function if there exists a continuous function $f: X\to[0,1]$ with $f(x) =0$ and $f(y)=1$.
