# Metric Topology

Let (*M*, *d*) be a metric space. Let $\mathcal{T}$ be the collection of all subsets of *M* that are open in the metric space sense with respect to the metric *d*.

$\mathcal{T} = \{ U \subseteq M \mid U \text{ is open in } (*M*, d) \}$

This forms a topology on *M*, called the metric topology on *M*, or the topology generated by *d*.

This collection $\mathcal{T}$ satisfies the axioms of a topology:
1.  $\emptyset \in \mathcal{T}$ and $M \in \mathcal{T}$.
2.  The intersection of any finite number of sets in $\mathcal{T}$ is also in $\mathcal{T}$.
3.  The union of any collection of sets in $\mathcal{T}$ is also in $\mathcal{T}$.
