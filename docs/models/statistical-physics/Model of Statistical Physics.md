Statistical physics is a large framework that applies to systems with large number (or maybe infinite) degrees of freedom, with the consideration that a ***statistical view*** of these systems would suffice (or perhaps is the best view possible).

In this model we cover the theoretical aspects and intuitions/philosophical considerations when working with statistical physics. The theory was predominantly a toolkit for studying particles yet in modern physics there's absolutely no place that we don't find a use of it.
