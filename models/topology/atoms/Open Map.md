# Definition

A map $f: X\to Y$ (continuous or not) is said to be an ***open map*** if it takes open subsets of $X$ to open subsets of $Y$; in other words, if for every open subset $U \subseteq X$, the image set $f(U)$ is open in $Y$. It is said to be a ***closed map*** if it takes closed subsets of $X$ to closed subsets of $Y$.
