General Relativity, is the physical theory explaining the phenomena known as gravitation. Gravitation, as our best theory suggests, the curvature of spacetime, a four dimensional [[Model of Manifolds|manifold]] we live in. Locally spacetime looks flat, and the flat spacetime, is governed by [[../special-relativity/Model of Special Relativity|Special Relativity]], the framework of inertial frames of reference. In this model we'd investigate the topic through a series of discussions, and developments. 

This model can be read on its own, but it is suggested that we have a look at other models before starting general relativity, as it is a mathematically dense topic.
