# Cox Theorem
“Instead of assigning probabilities to events, as in elementary probabilities, or to sets, as in the mathematical version (see Appendix 2.A), Cox gives a numerical value to the plausibility P( p | q) of a proposition p given that another proposition q is true.” (Bricmont, 2022, p. 12)
