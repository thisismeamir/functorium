# Declaration of Intent — CERN Visit


## Purpose

This document outlines the objectives I intend to pursue during my upcoming visit to CERN as a member of the Tehran FCC Group. The visit will be used to advance active technical work, establish new collaborative ties, and strengthen the team's long-term capacity for independent contribution to the FCC collaboration.

---

## Objectives

### 1. WMass Analysis — Progress and Milestones

The primary technical objective of this visit is to make meaningful and demonstrable progress on the WMass analysis project. I will use the time at CERN to work in close proximity with relevant collaborators, resolve outstanding technical blockers that are difficult to address remotely, and reach a concrete milestone in the analysis pipeline. The goal is to leave with results or deliverables that represent a clear step forward in the team's contribution to this analysis.

### 2. FCC Software Development — Connections and Proposals

I will engage with members of the FCC software community to identify concrete areas where the Tehran FCC Group can contribute to FCC software development. This includes exploring open tasks, upcoming development needs, and gaps where our team's involvement would be both feasible and valued. Where appropriate, I aim to define preliminary proposals or expressions of interest that can be followed up after the visit, laying the groundwork for a more structured and visible software contribution role for the team.

### 3. fTOP Support Network — People and Resources

The Tehran FCC Group is currently running fTOP, an internal technical on-boarding program aimed at training members to become self-sufficient in HEP analysis and software development. During the visit, I will actively seek out individuals within the FCC collaboration and broader CERN community who could support this initiative — whether through guest lectures, remote mentoring, code review, or other forms of engagement. The objective is to connect fTOP to the wider collaboration and reduce the program's dependence on a single internal resource.
