1. **Mathematics**
   This part would be our reference point to mathematical ideas, structures, and theories that shapes the theory of quantum mechanics. Physical theories are pairs of mathematics, with principles, and experimental facts that govern the math. Therefore this would be the part where we learn before making our principles and physical reality take the role of a governor.
2. **Elements**
   In this section we would start constructing what is known as the standard quantum mechanics. Which is given in most beginner level textbooks.
3. **Formalisms**
   This section would contain formalisms of quantum mechanics, such as the path integral, information theory, and statistical mechanics. 
4. **Landscapes**
   In this part we would discuss the theory in action, where it is being used, why and how.
5. **Dialect**
   This part would be solely a philosophical discussion, on interpretations, and the foundations of quantum mechanics.
