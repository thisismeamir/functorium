# Limit Point and Isolated Point

## Limit Points

Given a topological space $X$ and a set $A\subseteq X$, we say that ***a point $p\in X$ is a limit point of $A$*** if **every neighborhood of $p$ contains a point of $A$ other than $p$** which itself might or might not be in $A$.

Limits points are also called *accumulation points* or *cluster points*.

## Isolated Points

A point $p\in A$ is called ***an isolated point of $A$*** if $p$ has a neighborhood $U$ in $X$ such that $U\cap A=\{p\}$. Thus every point of $A$ is either a limit point or an isolated point, but not both.
