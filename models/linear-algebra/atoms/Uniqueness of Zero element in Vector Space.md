Okay, here are the proofs, formatted as requested:
Let $0_1$ and $0_2$ be zero elements in a vector space $V$. Then for any $v \in V$:

$0_1 + v = v$ and $0_2 + v = v$

Substituting $0_2$ for $0_1$ in the first equation:

$$0_2 + v = 0_1 + v$$

Since addition is commutative, $0_1 + v = v + 0_1$. Therefore:

$$0_2 + v = v + 0_1$$

By the additive identity property:

$$v + 0_1 = v$$

Subtracting *v* from both sides:

$$0_2 = 0_1$$

Hence, the zero element is unique.
