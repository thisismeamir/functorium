# Differential Forms

Members of the [[../linear-algebra/atoms/Dual Vector Spaces (Forms)|Dual Vector Spaces (Forms)]] are called forms. In general a $p$-form is a tensor of rank $(0,p)$. A $p$-form is a map that takes $p$ vectors and return a scalar:

**One Form**
$$
f: V\rightarrow \mathbb R
$$
**Two Form**

$$
f: V\times V\rightarrow \mathbb R
$$
And in general $p$-form:

$$
f: \underbrace{V\times V\times \dots \times V}_{p \text{ times}} \rightarrow \mathbb R
$$
