# The Unit Circle

The (unit) circle is the subset $S^1 \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2$ consisting of vectors in the plane with length 1:

$$ S^1 = \{x \in \mathbb{R}^2 : |x| = 1\} $$

It's often useful to identify the plane $\mathbb{R}^2$ with the set of complex numbers $\mathbb{C}$ by the correspondence $(x, y) \leftrightarrow x + iy$, and think of the circle as the set of complex numbers with unit modulus:

$$ S^1 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| = 1\} $$
