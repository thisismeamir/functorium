**FCC-ee physics case and CDR**

- FCC Collaboration, _FCC-ee: The Lepton Collider_, Eur.Phys.J.ST 228 (2019) — this is the Conceptual Design Report. Read the executive summary and Chapter 2 on physics performance. Available free on Inspire-HEP.
- Blondel & Janot, _FCC-ee overview and status_, arXiv:2106.13885 — a good compact overview

**Why precision matters**

- Peskin, _Estimation of LHC and ILC Capabilities for Precision Higgs Boson Coupling Measurements_, arXiv:1207.2516 — shows concretely how detector resolution translates to physics reach

**Reconstruction as inference**

- Frühwirth, Regler et al., _Data Analysis Techniques for High-Energy Physics_, Cambridge University Press — Chapter 1 gives the statistical foundations. This book will recur throughout the curriculum.

**The Key4hep ecosystem**

- Key4hep documentation: [https://key4hep.github.io/key4hep-doc/](https://key4hep.github.io/key4hep-doc/)
- EDM4hep: [https://github.com/key4hep/EDM4hep](https://github.com/key4hep/EDM4hep) — read the README and the data model description

**For genuine context**

- Watch: _CERN Academic Training — Detector Instrumentation_ lecture series on CDS (CERN Document Server). Specifically the lectures by Jochen Witteck on silicon detectors and Werner Riegler on detector physics.
- Watch: _PFA and ILD concept_ talks from LCWS conferences — Mark Thomson's talks from 2008-2012 explain PFA from first principles better than any paper
