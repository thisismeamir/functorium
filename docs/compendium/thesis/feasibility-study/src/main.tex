    \begin{abstract}
        The following document presents a feasibility study for a Master's proposal in the field of gravitational waves, numerical relativity, and data analysis.
        The study aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the current state of research in these areas, identify potential research questions, and outline the necessary resources and methodologies required to pursue this line of inquiry.

        This feasibility study examines the current state and future directions of gravitational wave physics, with particular emphasis on the computational and numerical challenges that lie at the heart of extracting physical insight from these observations.
        The study serves three primary objectives: first, to establish why gravitational wave research remains a vital and active frontier of fundamental physics; second, to demonstrate the field's current relevance through recent advances and ongoing developments; and third, to identify concrete research directions—specifically those involving numerical relativity and data analysis—that could form the basis of a master's thesis.
    \end{abstract}
