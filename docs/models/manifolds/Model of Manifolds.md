Manifolds are a special types of topological spaces (namely differential topological spaces). They are the foundations of many important subjects in physics and are necessary as the backbone of [[../general-relativity/Model of General Relativity|General Relativity]]. In this model we would try to understand, and theoretically go through the subject. As this is a mathematical subject that relies on other theories to be understood therefore we would first add the mathematical frameworks and theories that are somewhat preliminaries to manifolds.

- [[../topology/Model of Topology|Model of Topology]]
- [[../linear-algebra/atoms/Vector Spaces|Vector Spaces]]
- [[../linear-algebra/Model of Linear Algebra|Model of Linear Algebra]]

The latter two are there because is manifolds are to be used in physics then the notion of Tangent Spaces, and other topics heavily rely on them. Topology on the other hand is only necessary up until the basics.
